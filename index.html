📝 About Market Odds & Vig: Sportsbook odds include a built-in margin (vig/juice), meaning raw implied probabilities sum to more than 100%. The market probabilities shown here have been adjusted to remove the vig (normalized to sum to 100%) for a fair apples-to-apples comparison with the model. Odds shown are based on composite 2026 Milan-Cortina Olympic hockey futures from major sportsbooks (proxied from recent international tournament lines where 2026-specific odds are not yet widely available).
